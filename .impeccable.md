## Design Context

### Users

Vue developers and visual designers use dither-ui Studio equally. They arrange, configure, preview, and export real dither-kit components on an infinite canvas without needing to understand implementation details before they begin.

### Brand Personality

Precise, expressive, restrained. The interface should feel crafted and technical without becoming ornamental, generic, or intimidating.

### Aesthetic Direction

Dark, monospace, pixel/dither identity, worn as Y2K retrofuturism mixed with biomorphic design and technical sportswear: liquid chrome and a faint pearl sheen as the materials, hardshell panels with two cut corners holding soft gel wells, capsule keys, the kit's own liquid chrome as organic forms, and spec labels and slanted speed tags where a sportswear spec sheet puts them. The dither and the mono stay the signature; red stays out. The landing leads this look; the docs and Studio follow. The marketing landing speaks the same voice as the docs and Studio (dark tokens, monospace, hairlines), borrowing the structure of the 2000s oakley.com portal (dense panels in columns, lists under a heading) and the scale of the dither winners on Awwwards (full-bleed art bands, display-size type), with the kit shown live as its own visuals: one film in the hero, the others in the sections they show, and icons doing the work of labels (kit glyphs for ideas, the tools' own one-colour marks for npm, pnpm, Bun, Node, Vue, Svelte and the coding agents). Its copy follows the design.how bans the user picked (no uppercase labels, no eyebrows, no middle dots or em dashes, no arrow links) and no space is left empty; see `src/pages/AGENTS.md`. Studio takes interaction cues from Excalidraw: a canvas-first workspace, fast direct manipulation, minimal persistent chrome, contextual properties, and a searchable component library. Its chrome speaks the same site voice (monospace, hairlines, sentence-case labels, kit glyphs on every icon button, ringed pressed states; blue is reserved for canvas selection), so it remains recognizably dither-ui rather than visually cloning Excalidraw.

### Design Principles

- Keep the canvas primary; controls occupy space only when the current task needs them.
- Make insertion, selection, movement, duplication, zooming, and editing immediate and keyboard-accessible.
- Expose every public component as a functional demo; child-only pieces live inside the smallest valid composition.
- Serve beginners through clear defaults and progressive disclosure while preserving granular controls for developers.
- Preserve the dark dither identity, accessibility floor, reduced-motion support, and restrained visual hierarchy.
